### 👋 About

Software development applied to **intelligence systems for large distribution
centres** — the software layer that integrates management systems with the equipment
responsible for moving goods.

My work spans the whole lifecycle of a solution: from understanding the physical flow
alongside the operations teams, through development and rollout, to the infrastructure
that keeps the systems running.

> In logistics environments requirements rarely arrive documented — they emerge from
> understanding the physical flow that sustains the process.

<br/>
