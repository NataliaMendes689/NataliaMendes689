My work spans the whole lifecycle of a solution: from understanding the physical flow

that keeps the systems running.

## Main areas

- **APIs and integrations** between corporate systems and shop-floor systems
- **Microservices** for communication between automation equipment and software,
  adding intelligence to the operation
- **Databases** — modelling, migration and ongoing support
- **Infrastructure**, physical and systemic: server build-out, networking, containers
  and deployment environments
- **Internal tooling** to support technical teams

I consider proximity to the people operating the system essential. In logistics
environments requirements rarely arrive documented — they emerge from understanding
the physical flow that sustains the process.
